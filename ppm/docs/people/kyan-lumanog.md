## How he likes to work

- **Objective over agreeable.** He wants what's better for the project, with reasons, and push-back when he's wrong.
- **Evidence, not memory.** Check a claim against the code or the documentation; say what you couldn't check.
- **Plain, warm and short.** Answers, docs, commits and pull requests that read like a teammate talking. No em dashes.
- **Design taste:** calm, sleek, consistent; more blue, less green; never gradients, emojis, cards inside cards, pill badges or illustrations ([design-system.md](../guides/design-system.md)).
- **Fewest clicks, and scale:** the common path short; features that still work with ten times the people.
- **No clutter:** don't create files or folders nobody asked for; say where and why first.
