# Assigning work

> Draft for the in-app Handbook, shelf "Using the PPM". Written during the build as sample content (2026-10-01) and saved here on 2026-10-03. Some features came after it (repeating tasks, moving calendar blocks, an admin signing off in someone's place, sign-off requests going to the people the rule names), so check it against the app before publishing.

*Give every task one owner, and see who has room before you do.*

A task has exactly one assignee: the person who will move it to done. If two people share the work, split it into two tasks.

## How to assign

1. Open the task, or hover over it in a list.
2. Choose **Assignee**. The list shows how many open and overdue tasks each person has, so you can see who has room.
3. The person gets a notification in their inbox straight away.

In a list, move to a task with **J** and **K** and press **I** to assign it to yourself.

## When the work changes hands

Reassign the task. The history keeps who had it before, and both people are notified.
